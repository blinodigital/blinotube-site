BlinoTUBE

BlinoTUBE is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to publish their own videos, from their own computer, to their own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts, using captions prepared in advance.

It is not offered to the public. Only accounts owned by the creator are connected, and every account is authorized through the official TikTok and Google login screens.

What it does with TikTok

  • Lets the creator sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) to connect their own account.
  • Uploads the creator's own videos with the caption they wrote (Content Posting API).
  • Shows the creator the status of each upload.
