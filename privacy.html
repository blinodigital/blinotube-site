Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Who uses BlinoTUBE

BlinoTUBE is a private tool that runs only on its owner's computer and connects only accounts owned by that person. There are no other users.

2. Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload videos to that account.
  • The status of uploads made by BlinoTUBE.

3. How data is stored

All data is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server operated by BlinoTUBE, not shared with third parties, not sold and not used for advertising.

4. Data sent to TikTok

Only the videos and captions the account holder chooses to publish are sent to TikTok, through TikTok's official API.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. Disconnecting the account in BlinoTUBE deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

6. Children

BlinoTUBE is not directed to children.

7. Contact

Questions can be sent through github.com/blinodigital.
